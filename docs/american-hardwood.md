# American hardwood implementation

Verified commercial rules are supplied by the business owner in the implementation brief. `src/lib/american-hardwood.ts` is the reusable species/terms/FAQ catalogue; species characteristics are general guidance, not batch specifications. References: AHEC species guide and compare-species, Hardwood Information Center; Wormy Maple terminology checked against Penn State CVMS ambrosia maple collection and Wood Database. Birch identity is explicitly unconfirmed; yellow birch properties are conditional. No invented numerical mechanical values, stock quantities, grades, dimensions, certificates, prices or deposit requirements.

Representative AI grain contact sheet: original generated asset at `public/images/american-hardwood-reference.webp`; each species uses its corresponding panel. Prompt: unstained natural-grain reference sheet, three columns/four rows in catalogue species order, no branding. Clearly labelled as illustrative and not stock. No supplier imagery or contacts.

Forms hand off to WhatsApp; they do not persist leads or upload documents. BOQ is submitted as pasted line items or a document link; original files may be attached in WhatsApp. Sending the draft is required. Samples require availability and price confirmation.
